INREACTIVE SALES DASHBOARD 

Overview

This project presents an interactive sales dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI, with data prepared in Microsoft Excel. The dashboard analyses sales performance across product lines, countries, customers, deal sizes, and time periods.

Objectives

•	To evaluate overall sales performance using key performance indicators (KPIs).
•	To identify the highest-revenue product line, country, and customer.
•	To compare sales performance across quarters and months.
•	To examine monthly sales trends and potential seasonal patterns.
•	To provide actionable business recommendations based on the findings.


Tools Used

Microsoft Excel: Descriptive data analysis and summary statistics.
Microsoft Power BI: Data visualisation and interactive sales dashboard development.
GitHub: Project documentation and portfolio presentation.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales: 10.03 million
- Total Orders: 307
- Average Order Value: 32,680
- Number of Customers: 92
- Number of Countries: 19

Dashboard Features

- Interactive filters for year, quarter, country, and month.
- Monthly sales trend analysis.
- Sales distribution by deal size.
- Sales comparison by product line.
- Top 10 Customer-level sales performance.
- Country-level sales comparison.
- KPI cards for monitoring overall performance.

Key Findings

**Strong overall sales performance**: Total sales reached 10.03 million from 307 orders, supported by an average order value of 32.68K.
**Seasonal sales pattern**: The fourth quarter was the best-performing quarter, with approximately 3.87 million in sales. 
November was the strongest month, recording approximately 2.12 million, while June was among the weakest months. 
**Product concentration**: Classic Cars was the dominant product line, generating approximately 3.92 million, while Trains recorded the lowest sales at approximately 226K. 
**Deal-size concentration**: Medium-sized deals accounted for 61% of sales, followed by small deals at 26% and large deals at 13%.
**Country performance**: USA was the leading market, generating approximately 3.63 million in sales.
**Customer with highest revenue**: Euro Shopping Channel was the highest-performing customer among the top 10, contributing approximately 912K in sales.


*Note: These findings are based on the dashboard's displayed values and should be verified against the underlying dataset before publication.*
