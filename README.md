Interactive sales dashboard

Overview

This project presents an interactive sales dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI, with data prepared in Microsoft Excel. The dashboard analyses sales performance across product lines, countries, customers, deal sizes, and time periods.

The project aims to identify the highest-revenue product line, the country and customer contributing the most to sales, the best-performing quarter, the month with the lowest sales, and potential seasonal patterns. Interactive filters enable users to explore sales performance and gain insights to support data-driven business decisions.

Objectives

Evaluate overall sales performance using key performance indicators (KPIs).
Identify the highest-revenue product line, country, and customer.
Compare sales performance across quarters and months.
Examine monthly sales trends and potential seasonal patterns.
Provide actionable business recommendations based on the findings.

Tools Used

Microsoft Excel: Descriptive data analysis and summary statistics.
Microsoft Power BI: Data visualisation and interactive sales dashboard development.
GitHub: Project documentation and portfolio presentation.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales: $10.03 million
- Total Orders: 307
- Average Order Value: $32,680
- Number of Customers: 92
- Number of Countries: 19

Dashboard Features

- Interactive filters for year, quarter, country, and month.
- Monthly sales trend analysis.
- Sales distribution by deal size.
- Sales comparison by product line.
- Top 10 Customer-level sales performance.
- Country-level sales comparison.
- KPI cards for monitoring overall performance.

Key Findings

- Overall Sales: Total sales amounted to approximately $10.03 million across 307 orders.
- Product Line Performance: Classic Cars generated the highest revenue, at approximately $3.9 million.
- Country Performance: The United States recorded the highest sales, at approximately $3.6 million.
- Deal Size: Medium-sized deals contributed approximately 60.68% of total sales.
- Customer Performance: Euro Shopping Channel was the highest-contributing customer, with approximately $0.91 million in sales.
- Monthly Sales Trend: November recorded the highest monthly sales, at approximately $2.1 million, while June recorded the lowest, at approximately $0.5 million.
- Seasonal Patterns: Sales fluctuated across the year, with a notable increase towards October and November. Further analysis is required to establish whether this represents a consistent seasonal pattern.

*Note: These findings are based on the dashboard's displayed values and should be verified against the underlying dataset before publication.*

Recommendations

1. Strengthen high-performing product lines:** Maintain the availability of Classic Cars and investigate opportunities to improve the performance of lower-selling product lines.
2. Build on the US market: Maintain customer relationships in the United States and assess opportunities for growth in other countries.
3. Plan for peak sales periods: Prepare inventory, staffing, and marketing campaigns ahead of the observed October–November sales increase.
4. Investigate low-sales months: Examine the reasons for lower sales in June and consider targeted marketing or promotional strategies where appropriate.
5. Retain high-value customers: Strengthen relationships with major customers, particularly Euro Shopping Channel, through customer retention initiatives and personalised service.
6. Optimise deal-size strategies: Examine the factors contributing to medium-sized deals' strong performance and identify opportunities to improve revenue from other deal sizes.
7. Monitor sales performance: Use the dashboard regularly to track KPIs, identify changes in sales trends, and support evidence-based business decisions.

Conclusion

The dashboard provides an overview of sales performance across products, markets, customers, and time periods. It demonstrates how interactive data visualisation can help identify revenue drivers, monitor trends, and inform business planning.
